I’m finishing my Ph.D. at [Radboud University](https://www.ru.nl/) in the Netherlands, where I explore how AI can automate CI/CD workflow and improve software development process. Previously, I worked as a Research Engineer at [Huawei Technologies France](https://www.huawei.com/fr/). I’ve been coding for 8 years and teaching for the second half.

I enjoy taking vague problems, turning them into something concrete, and building practical solutions around them. After several years in research, I’m now looking to bring that mindset closer to real-world products as a **Software Engineer** or **Data Scientist**.

Outside of work, I enjoy swimming, bouldering, and exploring the world with my girlfriend.

At the end of the day, I’m just another human living on Earth.
